## Theme-to-Passage Tips

**必修3-1 (Festivals):** Contrast 2–3 world festivals. KPs: despite vs although, go off/fade away/set off, grateful/gratitude.

**必修3-2 (Moral/Virtue):** Biography of a moral exemplar. KPs: moral vs virtue vs principle, tend to vs be inclined to, a great deal of vs a large number of.

**必修3-3 (Diversity):** Chinatown or multicultural city profile. KPs: diverse/diversity, historical vs historic, apart from vs except for, bring about/result in/lead to.

**必修3-4 (Space):** Space exploration milestones. KPs: determined vs determination, so as to / in the hope of, carry on vs go on.

**必修3-5 (Law/Justice):** Victorian London or moral wager story. KPs: ought to vs should, in case, be about to, odd/bizarre/strange.
